How to use the Email tab

Use this tab to create a permanent, verifiable proof that a specific person sent you an email on a given date — before a deadline, before a decision, before a dispute. The stamp is stored on Arweave and cannot be altered or deleted. Anyone can verify it independently.

Enter your email address and an app password, then specify the sender and a date range. Click Fetch emails to retrieve matching messages, select one, and stamp it.

What you can prove

The stamp records the sender's address, the date, and a cryptographic hash of the message. It proves that a specific sender sent this exact message before the moment of stamping. It does not prove the content is true — only that it exists and was sent.

Gmail: create an app password

Gmail requires an app-specific password for IMAP — your regular Gmail password will not work here.

  1. Make sure 2-Step Verification is enabled on your Google account.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com → Security → App passwords.
  3. Choose Mail as the app and give it any name.
  4. Copy the 16-character password shown and paste it into the Password field above.

The app password gives read-only access to your email via IMAP. You can revoke it any time from the same page.

Finding the sender and date in Gmail

  1. Open the message in Gmail.
  2. The sender's address is shown next to the name in the From line — hover over the name to reveal the full address.
  3. The date is displayed at the top right of the message. For the exact timestamp, click the three-dot menu → Show original.
  4. Enter the sender address and a date range that covers that date, then click Fetch emails.
