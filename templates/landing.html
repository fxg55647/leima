Two guarantees, sealed together

What's certain
The document's hash is sealed on Arweave the moment you submit it. If the file is ever swapped or altered later, the mismatch is provable — this rests on the mathematics of hashing, not on the AI being right.
What's useful
Three independent AI passes — one for supporting evidence, one against, one to judge between them — produce a verdict on what the document actually shows, timestamped before anyone had reason to shape it.

You submit a document and a claim. Leima analyses it, then discards the document itself — only the hash and the verdict are sealed on Arweave, permanently and independently checkable by anyone.
